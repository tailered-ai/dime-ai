# DEF-028 — regenerating a run artifact invalidated closed-unit evidence

**Severity:** MEDIUM · **Detected by:** the ledger's own stale-evidence guard
**Authorized fix:** AMD-009

## DETECT

Immediately after closing DEF-023, `ledger verify` failed:

```
STALE_EVIDENCE: P05.EV01 -> docs/verification/evidence/p05/assurance.json (hash changed)
STALE_EVIDENCE: P05.EV01 -> docs/verification/evidence/p05/raw/assurance-run.txt (hash changed)
STALE_EVIDENCE: P05.EV02 -> docs/verification/evidence/p05/assurance.json.sha256 (hash changed)
STALE_EVIDENCE: P05.GATE01 -> docs/verification/evidence/p05/assurance.json (hash changed)
```

The guard worked exactly as designed. Nothing was corrupted; the ledger simply
refused to certify evidence that had moved under closed units.

## ROOT CAUSE

I recorded a **per-run artifact at a mutable path** as immutable unit
evidence. `assurance.json` is regenerated by every assurance run; the
authorized DEF-023/DEF-027 remediation legitimately produced a new one
(3 PROVEN + 1 FINDING_CONFIRMED → 4/4 PROVEN, `ASSURANCE_GREEN`), overwriting
the bytes three closed units were pinned to.

Unit status `PASS` is terminal by design, so those units could not be
re-closed against the new artifact, and the superseded bytes were not
reconstructable — the pre-remediation run no longer exists and its
`observational` section carried run-specific values.

## CORRECT

`supersede-evidence` on the sole ledger writer (AMD-009): an append-only
evidence move that **does not touch unit status** and **retains the superseded
paths and their original SHA-256 hashes forever** in a per-unit
`superseded_evidence` array. Reason, defect id, and replacement evidence are
all mandatory. Same shape as the append-only GEN-000 correction AMD-001
introduced for DEF-005.

Applied to `P05.EV01`, `P05.EV02`, `P05.GATE01`. Retained prior hashes:
`00862593…` (the pre-remediation artifact), `86e14fa1…`, `88022388…`,
`919769ab…`, `4edb99e8…`.

## RETEST

`ledger verify` exit 0. All three units remain `PASS`; each carries exactly
one supersession record naming DEF-028 and preserving its prior hashes.

## NEGATIVE RETEST

Three CLI refusals proven, each occurring **before any write**:
`SUPERSESSION_REASON_REQUIRED`, `SUPERSESSION_DEFECT_REQUIRED`,
`SUPERSESSION_EVIDENCE_REQUIRED`, plus `UNKNOWN_UNIT_ID`. A structural test
asserts every supersession on record carries a reason, a `DEF-*` id, and
64-hex prior hashes, and that status stayed terminal.

## CLOSE

Durable lesson: **evidence must be recorded at an immutable path.** A run
artifact is a product of a run, not a stable document — pinning one under a
closed unit guarantees a future authorized re-run will invalidate it. The
mechanism added here is a correction of record, not a loophole: it is
append-only, defect-linked, and visible in the rendered ledger.
